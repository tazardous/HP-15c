      About the emulation

      • Results are rounded to 10 significant digits, as on the real machine.
      • Memory is shared the HP‑15C way: 64 registers split between data registers, program lines, matrix elements and the imaginary stack. g MEM shows the split.
      • With the calculator off, press − then ON to reset Continuous Memory, or . then ON to swap the decimal point and comma. o is ON on your keyboard.
      • Conditional tests run from the keyboard show true or false. The RAN# sequence differs from a real 15C, and the self-tests are not emulated.
